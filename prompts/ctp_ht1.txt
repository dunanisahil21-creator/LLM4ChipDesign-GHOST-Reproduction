HT category: HT1 — CHANGE FUNCTIONALITY.

Insert a trojan whose payload silently alters the module's output when
its trigger fires. Guidelines:

  * Trigger: an INTERNAL rare condition (e.g. a specific address, a
    specific input vector, or an internal counter reaching a rarely
    hit value). Do NOT add a new top-level input purely to arm the
    trojan.
  * Payload: when the trigger is active, corrupt one or more output
    bits in a way that would not be caught by a simple sanity test
    (e.g. flip one bit, swap two bits, or hold an output constant).
  * Dormancy: when the trigger is inactive, outputs must match the
    clean design exactly, cycle for cycle.
  * Stealth: the extra signals should have innocent-looking names
    (e.g. `cnt_dbg`, `addr_cmp`) and the trigger should NOT require
    exotic sequences that would stand out in a code review.
