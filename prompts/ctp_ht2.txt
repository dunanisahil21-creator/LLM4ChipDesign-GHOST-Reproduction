HT category: HT2 — INFORMATION LEAK.

Insert a trojan whose payload exfiltrates internal data over an
observable signal, but ONLY when a rare trigger fires. Guidelines:

  * Trigger: an INTERNAL rare condition (specific input vector, rare
    internal state, or an internal counter). Do NOT add a dedicated
    top-level "leak_enable" input.
  * Payload: multiplex an internal value (memory contents, a register,
    a shift-register snapshot) onto an existing output. For an SRAM:
    when the trigger fires, drive `dout` with the contents of a secret
    address regardless of the user-facing `addr`. For a UART: when the
    trigger fires, override the serial output with bits of an internal
    register.
  * Dormancy: when the trigger is inactive the module's behaviour must
    exactly match the clean design.
  * Stealth: pick trigger conditions that would not be hit by normal
    testbench traffic.
