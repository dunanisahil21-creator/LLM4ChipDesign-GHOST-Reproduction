HT category: HT3 — DENIAL OF SERVICE.

Insert a trojan whose payload disables the module when its trigger
fires. Guidelines:

  * Trigger: an INTERNAL time-based or vector-based rare condition
    (an internal free-running counter crossing a specific value, or a
    specific input pattern applied twice). Do NOT add a top-level
    "kill" input.
  * Payload: when the trigger fires LATCH the design into a broken
    state — e.g. force the SRAM to read all-zeros forever, or force
    the UART to hold `tx` low (or high) and `busy` high so no further
    frames can be sent. The disable must persist until reset.
  * Dormancy: before the trigger fires the module's behaviour must
    exactly match the clean design.
  * Stealth: the extra counter / FSM should look like ordinary design
    housekeeping.
